# Logo 12-test battery

Run every mark through all 12 tests. Record pass or fail per test, with the evidence (a render, a screenshot, or a note). No test skipped. Iterate one variable at a time when a test fails.

1. Renders legibly at 16px (actual render, not a scaled-down guess).
2. Renders legibly at 32px.
3. Renders legibly at 48px.
4. Solid-black silhouette still reads as the mark.
5. Grayscale version still reads as the mark.
6. Inverted version still reads as the mark.
7. Circle crop keeps the mark intact.
8. Squircle crop keeps the mark intact.
9. Pasted into a real product screenshot, it belongs there.
10. On a competitor wall, it stands apart from the marks beside it.
11. Letterform audit: every letterform is clean and intentional.
12. Grid audit: the mark sits on its grid, with nothing off-grid by accident.
