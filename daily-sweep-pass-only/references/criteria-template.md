# Sweep criteria template

Copy the variant block that fits the sweep, fill it in, and keep it fixed
between runs. Update the cron job instructions in place when a rule changes.

## Stocks and crypto

Hard limits:

- $100 maximum per share or coin
- Focus on $20 to $100 names

A pick passes only when all three hold:

1. Analyst consensus target at least 20% above the price, with mostly Buy ratings
2. Earnings beat, revenue growing, or guidance raised
3. Not a one-day spike (check the trend)

Report per passing pick: price with as-of time and source, consensus target
with upside percent, analyst split, why it passed, risk, and next step.

## $0 services

Hard limits:

- Hard $0, no card required
- No phone or SMS verification

Report only new verified finds or losses, losses first. Dedupe every find
against the catalogue before reporting.

## $0 courses

Apply the same verification discipline as the services sweep: check the
provider's own pages, confirm the free route actually works, and record the
date of the check.

Known findings to carry forward:

- Coursera killed the full free audit on 2025-08-08
- The edX audit route is the usable door for free courses
