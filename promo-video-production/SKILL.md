# Promo video production

Produce promo videos from the real product only, cut to the sellable-ads structure, with real licensed sound and a caption in the user's own words. Use this skill whenever a product needs a promo video, a launch cut, or a social video.

## Film the real product, nothing else

- Drive the actual app in a capture rig, filming real use, such as typing, windows opening, and checkpoints being created.

- Use no still screenshots, no generated video, and no fake UI, because the product has to be seen performing.

- Show no people and no hands in the frame.

- Invent no copy, no rival, and no claim the product cannot back up, and use only the user's own words for any tagline.

## Cut to the sellable-ads structure

- Cut the 15-second version to the structure in `references/fifteen-second-storyboard.md`, with the hook in the first 2 seconds and the result in frame 1.

- Put 2 to 3 real UI beats in the proof section, from 2 to 10 seconds, so the viewer sees the product working.

- Include a trust beat from 10 to 12 seconds only when the claim behind it is verifiable, and leave it out when it is not.

- Close with the call to action from 12 to 15 seconds, "Free download, link in bio".

- Build 3 hook variants before cutting a 30-second version, so the hook is chosen on evidence, not on a guess.

- Judge the video by installs, not by views, and ship volume, because one cut is not a campaign.

## Use real sound, credited properly

- Use sound from real licensed recordings only, and write the CC BY credit line into the caption for every recording used.

- Never use synthesized audio, because it was rejected three times and the door is closed on it.

- Never use the user's own music unless the user names the track for that use, and pull a cut that used it without permission into a rejected folder.

- Normalize loudness to LUFS, and check the levels before delivery, so the cut plays at the same loudness as everything else.

## Deliver a fresh file and a paste-ready caption

- Give every cut a fresh filename, so the user never watches a cached old version by mistake.

- Write the caption with the user's words plus plain facts, and include the credit lines for the sound.

- Move a rejected cut to a rejected folder, and do not offer it again.

- When the user says they do not like a cut, stop guessing and ask what specifically is wrong before cutting again.

## Alternatives for better results

- Offer a teaser cut with no product shots at all when the launch needs mood before it needs proof.

- Ask the user to name the track when they want their own music in a cut, instead of picking one for them.
